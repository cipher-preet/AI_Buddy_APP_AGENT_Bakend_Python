## Rules
- No generic filler: “work harder”, “stay motivated”, “protect one deep-work block”, “make steady progress”.
- No ICP, pricing, lead-gen, or SaaS funnel language unless the goal is actually a business/product launch.
- Do not treat the final desired outcome as a weekly proof point.
- Personalized to starting level. A beginner and an experienced person must get different strategies.
